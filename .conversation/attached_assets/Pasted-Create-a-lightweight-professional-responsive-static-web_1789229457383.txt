IMPORTANT TECHNICAL CONSTRAINT:

This website must be extremely lightweight and easy to deploy through GitHub and Vercel.

Use:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript

Do NOT use React, Next.js, Vue, Angular or other heavy frameworks unless absolutely necessary.

Do NOT use Tailwind build tooling.

Do NOT use GSAP, Framer Motion, Three.js, Lottie or other large animation libraries.

Do NOT include node_modules.

Do NOT include build caches.

Do NOT include large binaries.

Do NOT include videos.

Do NOT include uncompressed photographs.

Do NOT include local font files unless absolutely necessary.
