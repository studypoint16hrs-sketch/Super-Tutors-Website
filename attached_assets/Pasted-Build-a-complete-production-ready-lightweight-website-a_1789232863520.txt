Build a complete, production-ready, lightweight website and lead-management workflow for a Bhopal-based home tutoring and tutor-assignment business.

BUSINESS BRAND:
SUPER TUTORS / SUPER TUTORS ACADEMY

IMPORTANT:
The business operates primarily as a home-tutor service and tutor assignment network in Bhopal. It serves TWO audiences:

1. PARENTS / STUDENTS
   - They need a suitable home tutor or online tutor.
   - They submit their tutoring requirement through a Google Form.

2. TEACHERS / HOME TUTORS
   - They want teaching assignments.
   - They register through a separate Google Form.
   - The business then matches suitable tutors with student requirements.

The website must therefore function as:
- A professional business website
- A lead-generation website
- A public assignment listing page
- A simple interface connecting Google Forms + Google Sheets + Google Apps Script
- A lightweight tutor-matching workflow

DO NOT build a complicated SaaS platform.
DO NOT build user accounts.
DO NOT build payment functionality.
DO NOT build a custom database.
DO NOT build authentication.
DO NOT build a complex CRM.

The website should remain extremely easy to deploy using GitHub and Vercel.

==================================================

Google Form

Google Sheet
      ↓
Google Apps Script
      ↓
Assignment Database / Matching Logic
      ↓
Public JSON Endpoint
      ↓
assignments.html

Status = Open
AND

22. FORM SUBMISSION AUTOMATION
==================================================

==================================================

DO NOT attempt to completely automate human decision-making.

Automate:

- data collection
- organization
- assignment ID generation
- filtering
- matching suggestions
- ranking
- notifications
- public assignment publishing
- interest collection

- tutor verification
- final tutor selection
- fee negotiation
- parent communication
- assignment confirmation
- disputes
- payment
- sensitive document verification

This is intentional.

The goal is to reduce manual workload, NOT eliminate the business owner's judgment.
